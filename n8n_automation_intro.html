⚡ WHAT "DONE" MEANS HERE

JSON handed back isn't done. Imported, credentials attached, test-executed, activated, and monitored — that's done. No workflow leaves my hands without an error-monitoring path attached, so it doesn't silently break again the week after I'm gone.

🐛 Bug Fixes & Rescue
+

Broken triggers, failed connections, silent errors, orphaned nodes. The most common call: "this used to work."

How it works: Read-only audit against the live workflow, tracing the execution log to isolate exactly where it's failing. Written findings report before anything is touched. Once approved, the fix goes in, gets test-executed, and I confirm it's running clean before calling it done.
📥 Lead Capture & CRM
+

Form fills in, gets qualified, lands in the CRM, and a follow-up goes out — with no human touch required.

How it works: A form submission or webhook triggers the workflow. A filter step checks it against your qualifying criteria, formats the data, and writes it into your CRM through its API. A personalized follow-up email fires automatically — no manual entry, no lead sitting untouched overnight.
💵 Invoice Chasing
+

Watches for overdue invoices, sends reminders, flags discrepancies, keeps payment status in sync.

How it works: The workflow watches your invoicing system on a schedule, flags anything past due, and sends a reminder using rules you set (how many days late, how many follow-ups, tone). Payment status syncs back to your project tracker automatically once it clears.
📅 Appointment Reminders
+

Confirmation, reminder sequence, self-serve reschedule — built into your existing booking system.

How it works: When an appointment is booked, the workflow schedules a reminder sequence — SMS, email, or both — timed however you want (24hr before, morning-of, etc.). If someone needs to reschedule, they can do it themselves through a link, no phone call back to your office required.
💬 Support Chatbots
+

Trained on your own docs and FAQs, with real escalation logic — not a generic bot with your logo on it.

How it works: The response layer is built on top of your actual docs and FAQs — not a generic model guessing. Escalation logic routes anything it shouldn't answer on its own straight to a human, and it connects into wherever your customers already are: website, SMS, or a messaging platform.
🩺 Maintenance Retainer
+

Monthly health report: what ran, what broke, what got fixed, what got updated. No abandoned projects.

How it works: An error-monitoring workflow watches everything built for you and alerts on failures before you'd notice them yourself. Every month you get a one-page report — what ran, what broke, what got fixed, what got updated — so "is this still working" is never a question you have to ask.

How I Handle Your Data

Credentials never pass through me

  • ✓ During diagnosis and design, I work with placeholder credentials and synthetic data — nothing real gets typed into chat or passed through an AI tool.
  • ✓ If live-credential testing is unavoidable, my default ask is a temporary, scoped API key — created by you, limited to what the fix requires, revoked by you the moment the fix is verified.
  • ✓ If your platform doesn't support scoped keys, verification happens inside your own n8n instance directly — I read the execution result and error output, never the raw key.
The boat-sailed rule. Before I bid on a build, I ask: does a productized platform already do this? If yes, I'll tell you up front — even if it costs me the job. My incentive is a working system, not a bigger invoice.
